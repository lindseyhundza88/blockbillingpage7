The Problem — Block Billing
You have been working on a court submission for 4.5 hours and logged it as a single entry.

This is not allowed. No single time entry may exceed 3 hours. This is called block billing — and it is not permitted.
⚠ A 4.5-hour entry must be split into two separate entries.
